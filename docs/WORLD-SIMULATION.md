# Full-world ecology and observation

- Uniform 0.5-second world steps process every active record in deterministic phases. Frame budgets stagger work, not behavioral quality. Unprocessed elapsed time and the mid-step cursor are saved. Existing game-fish counts are not increased during migration.
- Read-only completed-snapshot interpolation smooths rendering and follow-camera motion; water checks prevent visual shortcuts around corners. AI decisions retain their own timers. Selection and boat/camera distance do not schedule or simplify behavior.
- Shared 128 m prey index, cached species profiles, bounded navigation-route cache and target-change/path-exhaustion replanning reduce repeated searches. Rendering caches visible candidates and refreshes inspector text four times per second; it may cull/subsample distant markers.
- Habitat areas overlap and carry opportunities separately from mobile prey and depletable bottom forage. Physical search/detection/stalking/pursuit/strike/capture/consumption/recovery is shared by game fish and predators. Forage groups retain IDs through split/merge records. Species preferences, growth, hunger, routines and existing movement are retained.
- Fish Lab uses one selection, a toolbar and collapsible inspector. Empty clicks deselect unless explicitly locked. Following uses displayed positions only. Species-colored school markers show counts, headings, fear/spread and split/merge events. Close-range zoom presets are denser; imagery caching remains unchanged.
- Version 6 preserves scheduler debt/cursors, ecology and hunting state. Older saves receive a `before-world-scheduler` backup before first overwrite. The live launcher remains the current-source launcher; the Windows export is updated separately.

Focused checks passed for physical capture/no zone feeding, prey conservation and food-web save/load; and identical full-world outcomes under different frame budgets, boat/selection positions and mid-step save/load. No gameplay smoke runs, screenshots or performance benchmarks were performed. Maximum achievable acceleration/FPS is unmeasured; CPU-bound acceleration queues time and may run slower than requested. Habitat envelopes and biology remain approximations; narrow water excluded by the existing conservative navigation mask is still unavailable.
